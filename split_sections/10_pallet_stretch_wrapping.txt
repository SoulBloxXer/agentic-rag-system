# Which Machine Pallet Wrapper Should You Choose?

## Do You Need Stretch Wrap Or Machine Pallet Shrink Wrap?

When placing an order for a few tonnes of wrap for a machine pallet wrapper, it's important to distinguish between two entirely different types of polymer-reacting film. Otherwise, you're going to end up spending several hundred pounds on wrap that won't do your business reputation or product packaging any justice.

For eight out of ten clients, you are most likely seeking out pallet stretch wrap for distribution purposes. This is the most cost effective film and is typically made from polythene. Applied either by hand-held roll or via a semi-automatic machine pallet stretch wrapper, it stretches to hold products and boxes in place and protects from dust and moisture.

You can also use UV polythene stretch wrap to protect goods within from sunlight. Such is its multi-purpose polymer ability, you can find mixes that adapt to each situation, such as anti static stretch film for electronic goods and vented pallet wrap. Most businesses shipping a dozen crates a day tend to purchase a turntable stretch wrapper to save time on the distribution line.

The other 20% of businesses that may instead find machine pallet shrink wrap more useful would be fresh produce distributors. Slightly more expensive, the shrink wrap is made from Polyolefin and is breathable but also protects from moisture, dirt and dust too. Slightly more expensive and you will also need a manual held shrink gun to shrink the wrap around the boxes or crates on a pallet.

As a food producer, whether fresh fruit and veg, dairy and cheeses, or wholesale butchers, you will understand the need to use food grade shrink wrap wherever possible. A distributor or production end-of-line may find Polyolefin more manageable when dealing with smaller pallet loads as opposed to taller bulkier items which may find more strength in application from stretch wrap.

Depending on how large your operations are, you may opt for a mobile pallet wrapper, a semi-automatic stretch wrapper or an all out automated pallet system with conveyor belt system which has conveyors, palletiser and turntable. For machine shrink wrapping, pallets tend to be more small-scale but bespoke solutions can be built on demand.

# Machine Pallet Wrap vs. Hand Held Pallet Wrap

Shipping companies can struggle to package the warehouse consignment that needs to be transported. There are packaging problems that can result if the wrapping is not done properly. An efficient pallet wrap ensures that the pallet is safely packaged and delivered at the assigned time. Machine pallet wrap and hand held pallet wrap are the two types of pallet wrappers that are widely used by packaging businesses. The pallet wrappers have a stretched wrap film that is bound to a palletised rod. When the turntable of the pallet wrapper rotates, the film is placed tightly on the pallet. As the film touches the surface of the pallet, it contracts and binds the entire package together. If you are a business owner in need of a pallet wrapper, we mention a comparison of machine pallet wrapper with the hand-held pallet wrapper.

1.  **Safety considerations**

    The type of pallet wrapper used depends on the size of the pallet. If it is a small and light pallet, then a hand held pallet wrapper can be used. However, you need to be careful when rolling the film that the pallet is stable enough and held together. On the other hand, a machine pallet wrapper ensures that the entire pallet is wrapped thoroughly. It also enables the pallet to retain the balance that is essential for the safety of the employees working in the warehouse. If the pallet is loosely tied together, there can be accidents. To prevent the toppling of the pallet, a machine pallet wrapper needs to be used.

2.  **Cost**

    When delivering a consignment, the cost of the film that is used for wrapping is a large part of the overall material cost. Therefore, it is important to buy a film wrap that is capable of being stretched to the maximum limit. If a stretchable film is used, less film is needed to cover the same surface. This cost reduction can be easily achieved by using a machine pallet wrap as they can pre-stretch the material to a large extent. If the same pallet is wrapped using a hand held pallet wrap, the pre-stretch capability is reduced. Therefore, to save material costs, it is important that a machine pallet wrap is used.

3.  **Consistency of the wraps**

    It can be difficult for a warehouse employee to wrap a large pallet using a hand held pallet wrapper. This is because for large pallets it is important that every single pallet is wrapped in the same way. This consistency can be achieved through a machine pallet wrapper. A machine pallet wrapper ensures that the pallets are wrapped uniformly, thus reducing the likelihood of an inconsistently wrapped pallet.

4.  **Packaging time**

    If a hand held pallet wrap is used, the warehouse employee needs to spend more time in wrapping compared to the machine pallet wrapper being used. Using a machine pallet wrapper frees up the employee’s time and they can concentrate on other business activities. Additionally, the machine pallet wrapper works quickly so the work can be carried out efficiently.

# Machinery Guide: Pallet Stretch Wrap Machine

## Overview

A Pallet Stretch Wrap Machine is an essential tool for securely wrapping pallets of goods for storage and transport. This guide outlines the benefits of using a pallet stretch wrap machine, emphasizing its role in enhancing efficiency and ensuring product safety.

## Benefits of a Pallet Stretch Wrap Machine

### 1. **Enhanced Load Stability**

-   **Secure Wrapping**: Provides consistent and tight wrapping around pallets, reducing the risk of shifting during transport.
-   **Protection Against Damage**: Minimizes exposure to moisture, dust, and contaminants, protecting products from potential damage.

### 2. **Increased Efficiency**

-   **Automated Operation**: Streamlines the wrapping process, allowing for faster packaging compared to manual wrapping methods.
-   **High Throughput**: Capable of wrapping multiple pallets quickly, enhancing overall productivity in warehouse and shipping operations.

### 3. **Cost-Effective**

-   **Reduced Labor Costs**: Decreases the need for manual labor, allowing staff to focus on other tasks, which can lower operational costs.
-   **Material Savings**: Optimizes the use of stretch film, reducing waste and lowering packaging material expenses.

### 4. **User-Friendly Design**

-   **Intuitive Controls**: Features easy-to-use controls for adjusting wrap tension and speed, simplifying operation for users.
-   **Adjustable Settings**: Customizable wrapping parameters to accommodate different pallet sizes and product types.

### 5. **Improved Safety**

-   **Reduced Injury Risks**: Minimizes the physical strain on workers associated with manual wrapping, contributing to a safer work environment.
-   **Stable Pallets**: Ensures pallets are securely wrapped, reducing the risk of accidents during transport and handling.

### 6. **Versatility**

-   **Suitable for Various Products**: Can wrap a wide range of palletized goods, including boxes, bags, and irregularly shaped items.
-   **Customizable Wrapping Options**: Allows for the use of various stretch films, including colored and printed films for branding purposes.

### 7. **Space Efficiency**

-   **Compact Design**: Many pallet stretch wrap machines are designed to occupy minimal floor space, making them ideal for warehouses with limited room.
-   **Organized Storage**: Facilitates better organization of wrapped pallets, maximizing storage space and improving inventory management.

### 8. **Consistency and Quality**

-   **Uniform Wrapping**: Ensures that each pallet is wrapped consistently, providing a professional appearance and maintaining product integrity.
-   **Control Over Tension**: Allows for precise control of wrap tension, ensuring the appropriate level of stretch for different products.

## Conclusion

A Pallet Stretch Wrap Machine offers numerous benefits that enhance efficiency, reduce costs, and improve safety in packaging operations. By providing secure, consistent, and professional wrapping for pallets, it plays a crucial role in the logistics and supply chain processes.
