# Difference Between Types of Shrink Wrap

#### **Polyolefin Shrink Film**

Polyolefin shrink film is the superior choice for most shrink wrapping applications. It is known for its strength, clarity, and durability, making it ideal for wrapping individual products, multi-packs, and food items. Polyolefin is flexible and resistant to punctures, providing excellent protection during transit and a sleek, attractive presentation.

**Key Benefits of Polyolefin Shrink Film:**

-   Superior Clarity: Offers a glossy, professional finish that enhances product appearance.
-   Durability: Highly resistant to tears and punctures, making it reliable for a wide range of products.
-   Food Safe: Approved for direct contact with food products like meat and cheese, while providing tamper evidence.
-   Sustainability: Fully recyclable, unlike PVC, making it a better choice for environmentally conscious businesses.
-   Versatility: Can be used with L-sealers, shrink tunnels, and hand-held heat guns.

Polyolefin shrink film is available in centrefold rolls, making it easy to wrap and seal products. Once heat is applied, the film shrinks tightly around the product, providing a tamper-evident and protective package.

#### **Polythene Shrink Wrap Film**

Polythene shrink wrap film is primarily used for heavier-duty applications, such as securing large items or pallet loads. While it may not offer the same high clarity as polyolefin, it provides superior strength and durability, making it ideal for industrial use.

**Key Benefits of Polythene Shrink Wrap Film:**

-   Heavy-Duty Protection: Strong and durable, making it suitable for pallet loads and larger products.
-   Keeps Products Clean and Secure: Helps protect items during storage and transport by keeping them contained and dust-free.
-   Economical for Bulk Packaging: Ideal for wrapping large quantities or bulky items in distribution settings.

Polythene shrink wrap can be applied with a shrink tunnel or a hand-held heat gun. It is often used in sleeve-wrapping applications, where the product is partially wrapped but not fully enclosed.
